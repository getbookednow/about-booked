Example brief / illustrative

A Cape Town campaign day.

A fashion label wants to shoot a new collection over one day with a team of six. The brief calls for two nearby locations, a local photographer and stylist, and transport between sets.

BOOKED. would scope location research, local supplier options, a shoot schedule and production coordination. Talent, location fees, transport and other supplier costs would be agreed separately in writing.

For planning and coordination. Suppliers, travel and stay are paid separately, at cost.
