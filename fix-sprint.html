What it is

The gap is rarely the work.
It’s the read.

Most working creatives don’t have a talent gap. They have a legibility gap — distance between the level they’re operating at and what their digital presence communicates to the people deciding.

Usually it comes down to one thing. A lead image signalling the wrong category. Credits that exist only in tagged posts. A contact path that dies in a DM. These are structural gaps, not personal ones — and one of them is doing most of the damage.

Scope may include

The one doing
most of the damage.

  • Portfolio sequencing — the lead image and the first three, which decide what category you read as before anything else is seen.
  • Category signal — when the book still reads commercial and you’re being seen for editorial, or the other way round.
  • Credits — moved out of tagged posts and captions into something a casting platform or search tool can actually retrieve.
  • Contact friction — the path between someone deciding to book you and being able to, without going through a DM request.
  • Bio structure — role, market, and level, legible in the ten seconds anyone actually gives it.
  • Machine-readability — schema and structure, so the first filter can parse you at all.
  • A focused one-page layer, where there is no usable page to fix.

How it works

Named, closed, live.

  1. Scope

    A short conversation to name the one gap and agree exactly what changes. Written down before anything is paid, so there is no ambiguity about what you are buying.

  2. Fix

    Done against the agreed scope. You are not managing it, re-briefing it, or sitting through rounds of concepts.

  3. Ship

    The change goes live. You keep it — it works without me afterwards.

Questions

Before you commit.

What counts as one friction point?

One named gap — sequencing, category signal, credits, contact friction, bio structure, or machine-readability. Scope is agreed in writing before payment, so both sides know exactly what changes.

How is this different from a Portfolio Audit?

The audit is the read — it scores your presence and names which gap is costing you most. The Fix Sprint closes one. If you don’t already know which one it is, start with the audit: a sprint aimed at the wrong gap is wasted money, and I’d rather tell you that now.

Do I need my own website?

No. It can be applied to an existing site, a portfolio page, an agency profile, or a focused one-page layer built for the purpose.
