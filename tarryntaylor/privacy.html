Privacy Notice

Last updated 11 November 2026

This notice explains how Tarryn Taylor (“I”, “me”) handles your personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). I am the responsible party. Contact: tarryn.taylor@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • What you send me through the booking form, WhatsApp or email: your name, Instagram or portfolio link, contact details, what you want shot, dates, budget, and anything else you choose to include.
  • Photographs and video of you, if we shoot together.
  • Payment details needed to invoice you and record payment.

What this website collects

The booking form does not store or send anything to this website. Your answers open as a message in WhatsApp or your email app, and nothing reaches me unless you send it.

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It sets no cookies and does not identify you. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address when you load a page.

Why I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, quote, and book your shoot.
  • To deliver your images and keep a record of the work.
  • To invoice you and keep the financial records the law requires.
  • To show work in my portfolio, on the terms in my booking terms.

I don’t sell your information, and I don’t add you to marketing lists without asking.

Who else handles it

WhatsApp (Meta) and email (Google) carry our messages, and I use cloud storage to deliver and back up images. Some of these services store data outside South Africa, under their own privacy terms and safeguards.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don’t become bookings are deleted once they are no longer needed. Booking and payment records are kept for as long as tax law requires. Images are kept in my archive unless you ask me to delete them, subject to those records.

Images of you

I publish images of people only with their consent. If you appear on this site and want an image removed, email me and I will take it down.

Your rights

You can ask me what personal information I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to how I use it. Email tarryn.taylor@gmail.com. If you’re not satisfied with my response, you can complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.
