New Faces

Clean, natural first images for newly signed models.

Who it’s for

For models just starting out with an agency who need simple, honest images that show who they are. No heavy styling, no overdone retouching. Just a clear look at the face, the skin and the personality, so agencies and clients can see the model properly.

What’s included

  • One look
  • About one hour on set
  • Minimal natural makeup and styling
  • Posing guidance throughout the shoot
  • Time to ask any industry questions
  • 6 edited images
  • Shot in Cape Town

Price

R1,500

Agency bookings welcome. Ask about booking several new faces together.

About Tarryn

Tarryn Taylor is a Cape Town photographer with over 20 years in the fashion industry. She works closely with agencies and their new talent, and helps first time models feel at ease on set.
