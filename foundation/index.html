Foundation

What we're building.

Booked is setting up a Foundation to give young South Africans between 18 and 25 a laptop, data, and a findable creative portfolio. We start with a pilot of 10 to 20 people.

From Luca

I spent ten years working as a model before I understood what infrastructure actually does for a creative career. Most young creatives in South Africa don't have a laptop, consistent data, or a portfolio that anyone can find. This is the piece I want to build next.
